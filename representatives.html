FAQ

Questions Consultants Ask Us

Do I have to sell or deliver anything?

No. You make warm introductions and stay in the loop. Gershon Consulting runs discovery, the proposal and the US campaign delivery.

How am I paid?

You earn a referral share on the revenue from each client you introduce. We set the exact terms with you on the intro call and put them in writing before your first introduction.

Is it exclusive?

We aim for one representative per country (or per region in large markets), so you are not competing with other representatives for the same companies.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no fee to join and no minimum number of introductions.

Why only US citizens?

Companies trust advice about the US market more when it comes from an American who also knows their country. That dual perspective is the point of the program.
